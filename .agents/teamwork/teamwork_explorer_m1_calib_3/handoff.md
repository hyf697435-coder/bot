## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Premise**: `utils.atr` implements Wilder's RMA formula:
   $$\text{TR}_t = \max(H_t - L_t, |H_t - C_{t-1}|, |L_t - C_{t-1}|), \quad \text{ATR}_t = \text{RMA}(\text{TR}_t, 14)$$
   Empirical testing confirmed `utils.atr` is mathematically correct. On BTC ($70k-$76k), 5m ATR is ~213 USDC (0.28% - 0.31%). On ETH ($1900-$2250), 5m ATR is ~1.5 - 8.0 USDC (0.08% - 0.35%) during normal hours, and ~14.0 USDC during heavy market drops.
2. **Inference 1**: The value `213.72628640898196` in `state.json` was generated by a Bitcoin 5m candle calculation when the bot was trading BTC on 2026-11-06 (matching `risk_state_aggressive.json`'s BTC positions at entry $76,143.2).
3. **Inference 2**: When the BTC position exited, `_register_closed_position` reset other position parameters but forgot to reset `self.cached_atr_5m`.
4. **Inference 3**: During subsequent IDLE cycles, `self.coin` became `ETH`. Line 582 calculated `atr_14` for ETH, but `main.py` did not assign `self.cached_atr_5m = atr_14`. Consequently, `self.cached_atr_5m` remained frozen at BTC's 213.72.
5. **Inference 4**: `_persist_state()` wrote `self.coin: "ETH"` and `cached_atr_5m: 213.72628640898196` into `state.json`. `_load_state()` loaded this contaminated value back into memory.
6. **Inference 5**: Downstream consumers (`dashboard.py`, `main.py:_refresh_hard_stop`, `strategy.py:calculate_position_size`) read 213.72 as ETH's ATR, distorting stop loss distances by >20x and collapsing position sizing to the $10 minimum.
7. **Resolution**:
   - Provide a flexible, multi-input `utils.atr` (supporting both `DataFrame` and `(highs, lows, closes)`) along with standard aliases `rma_atr` and `wilder_rma_atr`.
   - Implement `strategy.calc_atr` with volatility sanity bounds: if $ATR / Price > 4.0\%$ or $< 0.02\%$, clamp or fall back to proportional volatility ($Price \times 0.0020$).
   - Update `main.py`: update `self.cached_atr_5m = atr_14` in `_idle_cycle`, reset it in `_switch_coin` and `_register_closed_position`, and sanitize it in `_load_state`.
   - Update `multi_coin_router.py`: record each coin's specific ATR in `radar_scores`.
   - Sanitize `dashboard.py`: guard `est_atr` against cross-coin distortion.
   - Clean `state.json`: reset `cached_atr_5m` to calibrated ETH value.

## 3. Caveats

1. **Market Volatility Regimes**: During acute flash crashes or pumps, ETH 5m ATR can briefly exceed 8.0 USDC (e.g. 14.0 USDC today when ETH fell -4.7%). The sanity bounds must allow real high-volatility expansions up to 4.0% of price without premature truncation, while firmly blocking catastrophic distortions like 11%+.
2. **Multi-Coin Radar Concurrency**: In multi-coin mode, `MultiCoinRouter` evaluates BTC, ETH, SUI, and ARB in the same cycle. Storing each coin's individual ATR inside `radar_scores[coin]["atr"]` ensures that multi-asset monitoring never confuses one asset's ATR with another.

### 5.3 Invalidation Conditions
This analysis and proposed calibration would be invalidated if:
1. Ethereum traded at $100,000+ where a 5m range of $213 represents <0.3% of price (empirically false; ETH is ~$1900-$2300).
2. The trading bot deliberately used a 1-day timeframe for 5m trading FSMs (disproved by `PROJECT.md` specifying 5m candles and 14-period Wilder RMA).
